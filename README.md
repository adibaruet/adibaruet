## 🍃 About Me

I'm a final-year B.Sc. student in **Electrical & Computer Engineering** at **RUET**. I like turning research ideas into things that actually work, from training models to building the backend and interface around them.

- 🔬 My thesis is on **Vision-Language Models**, focused on making visual question answering safer and more efficient for visually impaired users
- 💻 I also build backend systems, databases, and web apps
- 🚀 I care about AI that is reliable enough to use in the real world, not just good on a benchmark
- 🌱 Always learning something new

---

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- **Thesis research:** a two-stage approach to make VQA answers more trustworthy
- **Healthcare ML:** ECG-based atrial fibrillation detection
- **Web development:** full-stack projects with Next.js and React
